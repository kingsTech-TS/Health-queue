[261] Style: Normal | Stage 0: Pre-Upload and Account (Admin uploads students → Student verifies identity and creates account during active registration period)

[263] Style: Normal | Stage 2: Payment Upload and Verification (Student uploads receipt → Cloudinary stores securely → AI Gateway extracts RRR/reference, amount, date, and institution returning likely_valid/likely_invalid/needs_manual_review decision → Admin confirms or rejects in dashboard → passport and signature uploads unlock only after confirmation)
[264] Style: Normal | Stage 3: Document Uploads (Passport photograph with optional Cloudinary AI background removal; signature image)
[265] Style: Normal | Stage 4: Lab Request Form — Student Information Section (student fills surname, first name, age, sex, faculty, department; unique LAB-YYYY-NNNNNN number auto-assigned)
[266] Style: Normal | Stage 5: LAB_TEST Queue Session (student joins eligible queue → Lab Attendant calls → Laboratory section completed by staff: hospital/lab numbers, specimen, diagnosis, tests, antibiotic sensitivity, doctor signature, scientist name, dates received/reported)
[267] Style: Normal | Stage 6: Medical Examination Questionnaire (full illness/surgery/admission history lists + yes/no checklist for tuberculosis, diabetes, epilepsy, sickle cell, asthma, psychiatric illness, visual impairment, STD; menstrual disorder field for female students only; allergies, medications, family history)
[268] Style: Normal | Stage 7: PHYSICAL_REGISTRATION Queue Session (student joins → Registering Nurse calls → Physical Examination Form filled: weight, height, visual acuity, hearing, all body systems observations, laboratory findings summary, diagnosis, remarks)
[269] Style: Normal | Stage 8: Student Case Notes / Pink File (auto-created by system or staff; generates HC-NNNN health center number; captures personal info, next of kin with relationship and GSM, religion, matric number, home town/state, sex, date of registration; plus medical data: sensitivity, blood group, rhesus factor, genotype, hepatitis A/B, BP, 7-entry immunizations dict)
[270] Style: Normal | Stage 9: Registration Completed → Digital Health Center Card generated and available for download/printing
[271] Style: Normal | Rules: A student must complete Stage 1 before any other stage. A student must have payment confirmed before Stage 3, 4, or any queue. A student must complete Stage 4 before Stage 5. A student must complete Stage 5 and 6 before Stage 7. Progression is strictly controlled by eligibility checks in QueueService and at each form endpoint.
[272] Style: Heading 2 | 3.10 Missed Queue and Form Lock Policy
[273] Style: Normal | Queue Miss Handling: If a student is marked missed by authorized staff during an active queue session their current queue entry is flagged is_missed=True with status=missed, their queue_number is reassigned to the count of remaining non-missed entries + 1, and a new waiting entry is appended at the end of the queue. Students never lose their place entirely; fairness is preserved by moving missed students to the rear rather than dropping them.
[274] Style: Normal | Medical Form Read-Lock: Once a student submits a medical form (Basic Info, Lab Request Student Info, Medical Questionnaire) the document becomes read-locked for that student account. Subsequent GET or PUT attempts from the student role return a 403 lock message advising contact with admin for any needed corrections. Only staff or admin roles may update form contents post-submission, preserving record integrity for the Pink File.
[275] Style: Normal | Staff Suspension: If a staff member's account is suspended the system updates four fields atomically in the same $set operation: is_suspended=True, suspension_reason, suspended_by (actor ObjectId), suspension_date (UTC now). All future endpoint calls fail 403 with descriptive reason message. Unsuspension reverses is_suspended and clears related fields.

[410] Style: Normal | The principal software components are Python 3.11+, FastAPI, MongoDB Atlas, Motor, Cloudinary, JWT security libraries, bcrypt, Pydantic v2, an HTTP client for AI Gateway communication, and a modern web browser. FastAPI also provides Swagger UI and ReDoc API documentation.
[411] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3 System Implementation
[412] Style: Normal | Implementation followed the architecture and workflow specified in Chapter Three. The system was organized into authentication, student management, staff management, registration-period management, payment verification, medical forms, queue management, audit logging, and health-card generation modules.
[413] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.1 Authentication and Authorization Module
[414] Style: Normal | The module provides account registration and login for Admin, Student, Registering Nurse, and Lab Attendant users. Student registration is restricted to pre-uploaded student records. JWT bearer tokens identify authenticated users, while role-based dependencies restrict protected operations. Staff accounts require administrative approval.
[415] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.2 Student Management Module
[416] Style: Normal | Administrators can upload student records through CSV or add students manually by level from 100-level to 600-level. Registration numbers are checked against the pre-uploaded records and level-specific format validation is applied before account creation.
[417] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.3 Registration Period Module
[418] Style: Normal | Administrators can create, update, list, and delete registration periods for individual student levels. Student onboarding is permitted only when the relevant registration period is active.
[419] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.4 Medical Form Module
[420] Style: Normal | The system implements the staged Basic Information, Lab Request, Medical Examination Questionnaire, Physical Examination, and Student Case Notes (Pink File) workflow. Student-submitted forms are read-locked after submission, while authorized staff and administrators can make controlled updates where required.
[421] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.5 Payment Verification Module
[422] Style: Normal | Students upload payment receipts through the application and files are stored in Cloudinary. The payment review service can submit receipt information to the configured AI Gateway and receive a structured decision such as likely valid, likely invalid, or needs manual review. Final confirmation or rejection remains an administrative action.
[423] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.6 Queue Management Module
[424] Style: Normal | The system implements LAB_TEST, PHYSICAL_REGISTRATION, and PHYSICAL_EXAM queue types. Authorized staff create sessions with a title, time window, capacity, and estimated time per student. Eligible students receive sequential queue numbers. Staff can call the next student, mark attendance, or mark a student as missed; missed students are moved to the rear of the queue.
[425] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.7 File Management Module
[426] Style: Normal | Cloudinary stores passport photographs, signatures, and payment receipts while the database stores their corresponding URLs and public identifiers. This separates media objects from transactional records.
[427] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.8 Audit Logging Module
[428] Style: Normal | Administrative and staff actions are recorded in activity logs containing actor and staff identifiers, actor name, role, action, target, details, metadata, and creation time. This provides traceability for privileged operations.
[429] Style: Heading 2 | 4.3.9 Digital Health Center Card
[430] Style: Normal | After the required registration stages are completed, the system generates a digital Health Center Card containing the student's passport photograph, Health Center number, and registration information. The student can view, download, or print the card.
[431] Style: Heading 2 | 4.4 Database Implementation
[432] Style: Normal | MongoDB Atlas is the primary database. Major collections include users, students, staff, basic_info, lab_request_forms, medical_examination_questionnaires, physical_examination_forms, student_case_notes, queues, queue_sessions, registration_periods, and activity_logs. ObjectId references maintain relationships between users, students, staff, forms, queues, and sessions.
[433] Style: Heading 2 | 4.5 Security Implementation
[434] Style: Normal | Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Pydantic schemas validate request and response data, input sanitization reduces XSS and NoSQL-injection risks, medical-form read-locks protect submitted records, and suspended staff accounts are prevented from using protected operations.

[454] Style: Normal | The study addressed challenges associated with manual and walk-in-based student medical registration at Ekiti State University. The existing process involved physical queues, manual records, limited visibility of student position, difficulty coordinating registration stages, and administrative challenges during periods of high student demand. The literature review examined appointment scheduling concepts and existing healthcare appointment systems, while the system analysis identified the need for a localized workflow tailored to EKSU.

[456] Style: Normal | The implemented system provides student verification, registration, payment receipt submission and review, document upload, laboratory processing, medical questionnaire submission, physical examination, Pink File completion, queue management, audit logging, and digital Health Center Card generation. Four operational roles are supported: Admin, Student, Registering Nurse, and Lab Attendant.

[458] Style: Normal | The implemented design and functional testing produced the following findings:
[459] Style: Custom Number | 1. Pre-uploaded student records provide an identity-verification point before account creation.
[460] Style: Custom Number | 2. Registration-period controls restrict onboarding to configured periods and levels.
[461] Style: Custom Number | 3. Dependency checks prevent students from skipping required registration stages.
[462] Style: Custom Number | 4. Session-based queues provide controlled capacity and sequential processing.
[463] Style: Custom Number | 5. Role-based authorization separates administrative, student, nursing, and laboratory responsibilities.
[464] Style: Custom Number | 6. Read-locks and audit logs improve record integrity and traceability.
[465] Style: Custom Number | 7. Cloudinary separates media storage from transactional database records.
[466] Style: Custom Number | 8. AI-assisted payment review provides decision support while final confirmation remains an administrative action.
[467] Style: Custom Number | 9. Digital Health Center Cards provide students with a reusable record of completed registration.
